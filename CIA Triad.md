> The CIA Triad is a fundamental model of information security.

## 1. What is the CIA Triad?

The CIA Triad is a security model consisting of three principles:

1. Confidentiality
2. Integrity
3. Availability 

It helps organizations protect their information and systems.

## 2. Three Core Principles

### A. Confidentiality

**Definition:** Ensuring that information is accessible only to authorized individuals.

**Example:**

- Password protection.
- Data encryption.
- Access permissions.

**Security Question:** Who can access this information?

### B. Integrity

**Definition:** Ensuring that information remains accurate, complete, and unchanged without proper authorization.

**Example:**

- File integrity checks using SHA-256.
- Digital signatures.
- Database access controls.

**Security Question:** Has this information been modified without authorization?

### C. Availability

**Definition:** Ensuring that information and services are accessible when required by authorized users.

**Example:**

- Backups.
- Redundant servers.
- DDoS protection.

**Security Question:** Can authorized users access this service when needed?

## 3. CIA Triad Summary

|Principle|Main Objective|Example|
|---|---|---|
|Confidentiality|Protect information from unauthorized access|Encryption|
|Integrity|Prevent unauthorized modification|SHA-256 verification|
|Availability|Keep services accessible|Backups|

## 4. Real-World Example

Consider an online banking application.

- Confidentiality: Only the account owner can view their account details.
- Integrity: Nobody can secretly modify the account balance.
- Availability: Customers can access banking services when needed.
