## 📌 What is Spyware?
**Spyware** is a type of malware that installs itself on a device without the user's knowledge or consent. Its primary goal is to quietly monitor user activity, gather sensitive information, and transmit that data back to the attacker. 

Unlike ransomware or viruses, spyware aims to remain completely invisible and silent for as long as possible.

---

## ⚙️ How It Steals Data
Spyware tracks and captures your digital footprints using different built-in mechanisms:

* **Keylogging:** Recording every single keystroke made on the keyboard to capture usernames, passwords, and credit card numbers.
* **Screen Scraping:** Taking periodic screenshots of your desktop or active application windows.
* **Data Harvesting:** Scanning your local hard drives for browser cookies, saved passwords, chat histories, or personal documents.
* **Media Hijacking:** Turning on webcams or microphones to record audio and video without lighting up indication indicators if possible.

---

## 📂 Common Variants
* **Keyloggers:** Specialized software or hardware dedicated purely to tracking keystrokes.
* **Adware:** While primarily designed to serve aggressive pop-up ads, it frequently tracks browser habits to build ad profiles.
* **Info stealers:** Targeted malware meant to rapidly scoop up browser-saved passwords and crypto wallets, then exit instantly.
