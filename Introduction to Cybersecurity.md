> Understanding the foundation of digital security.

## 1. What is Cybersecurity?

Cybersecurity is the practice of protecting computers, networks, applications, and data from unauthorized access, attacks, damage, and theft.

## 2. Why is Cybersecurity Important?

- Protects personal information.
- Prevents unauthorized access.
- Protects organizations from cyberattacks.
- Maintains data confidentiality and integrity.
- Ensures availability of digital services.

## 3. Core Objectives

The three fundamental objectives of information security are known as the CIA Triad.

- [[CIA Triad]]

## 4. Important Terminology

|Term|Meaning|
|---|---|
|Threat|Potential cause of harm|
|Vulnerability|Weakness in a system|
|Exploit|Technique used to take advantage of a vulnerability|
|Risk|Potential for loss or damage|
|Malware|Malicious software|
|Phishing|Deceptive attempt to steal information|

## 5. Real-World Example

Consider a computer protected by a password.

- Threat: An attacker trying to access the computer.
- Vulnerability: A weak password.
- Exploit: Attempting to guess the password.
- Risk: Unauthorized access to personal files.
